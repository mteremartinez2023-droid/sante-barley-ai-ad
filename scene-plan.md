## Editing rules
- Keep a consistent studio background, lighting, and clothing across presenter shots.
- Interleave presenter segments with relevant B-roll; do not use unrelated luxury imagery that implies guaranteed wealth.
- Show figures long enough to read. Do not alter the user's amounts or percentages.
- Add captions in short phrases, synchronized to the voice. Use high contrast and safe margins.
- Use approved Santé logos, product photos, and official compensation-plan graphics only.
- Do not show fake testimonials, fabricated earnings screenshots, unverified health claims, or guaranteed-income language.

## Mandatory end-card / on-screen disclaimer
“Income and incentives are not guaranteed. Results depend on sales, qualifications, individual effort, and the official compensation plan.”

## Pre-publication verification
Confirm package contents and prices, commission ranges, promo validity, bonus rules, eligibility and qualification language against current official Santé materials. The script's figures are preserved as supplied; this guide does not independently verify them.
