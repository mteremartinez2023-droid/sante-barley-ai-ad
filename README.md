# Santé Barley AI Advertisement Project

A vertical 9:16 promotional video project for a global online business opportunity.

## Creative direction
- Format: vertical 9:16, target 1080 × 1920
- Runtime: approximately 3 minutes 30 seconds
- Presenter: supplied female presenter image, animated with a licensed AI-avatar/video tool
- Voice: natural Filipino female voice; preserve the Taglish script
- Visual style: premium global-business studio, presenter desk shots, clean product and business B-roll
- Brand palette: green, navy, white, and restrained gold
- Captions: bold, readable Filipino/English subtitles

## Important production notes
1. Preserve all package prices, commission percentages, bonuses, and other figures exactly as supplied in the script.
2. Use authentic Santé product imagery and packaging. Do not fabricate package designs, logos, endorsements, or product claims.
3. Do not present income as guaranteed. Results depend on sales, qualifications, effort, and the official compensation plan.
4. Include a brief on-screen disclaimer: “Income and incentives are not guaranteed. Results depend on sales, qualifications, individual effort, and the official compensation plan.”
5. Avoid unsupported medical or disease-treatment claims about Santé Barley or other wellness products.
6. Confirm all compensation-plan details and product claims against current official company materials before publishing.

## Project files
- `script.md`: supplied voice-over script
- `scene-plan.md`: visual and editing plan
- `index.html`: browser-based production brief and script viewer

## Rendering
This repository stores the creative brief and production assets. GitHub Pages can publish the project page, but realistic image-to-video animation, voice generation, and final MP4 rendering require a video-generation service (for example, HeyGen or another compatible editor).
